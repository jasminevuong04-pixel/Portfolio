Medical Delivery UAV

CAD lead for a fixed-wing UAV designed for rapid medical delivery within the "golden hour." 4.5 lb payload over a ~30 nm round trip. Built airfoil geometry from coordinate data using splines — NACA 6412 main wing, NACA 0012 stabilizers.

03 / 04
Aerodynamic Analyst

CFD Airfoil Analysis

CFD study in COMSOL Multiphysics (MAE 150B). Simulated flow over a 2D cylinder, NACA 0010, NACA 4410, and a 3D finite wing. Analyzed lift, drag, wake structures, and wingtip vortices. Studied the impact of mesh refinement on simulation accuracy.

COMSOLCFD Lift & DragMesh Analysis3D Wing

Simulation Cases

  • 2D cylinder: wake formation and drag behavior vs mesh density
  • NACA 0010 symmetric: α = 0° to 10°, lift vs thin airfoil theory
  • NACA 4410 cambered: higher CL at all angles including α = 0°
  • 3D finite wing (NACA 4410): wingtip vortices and induced drag

Key Findings

  • Mesh refinement critical — significantly affected drag and lift accuracy
  • Viscous effects and low Re reduced lift below thin airfoil theory
  • Cambered airfoils: higher drag at low angles — clear lift/drag tradeoff
  • 3D induced drag clearly visible in finite wing simulation
  • Computational setup as important as physical modeling

About Jasmine

I'm an Aerospace Engineering student at UCLA with a passion for turning complex engineering requirements into manufacturable, high-performance designs. My work spans the full design lifecycle — from initial concept and CAD modeling to structural analysis, simulation, and fabrication.

I've served as CAD lead and systems lead on projects ranging from multi-stage rockets and medical delivery UAVs to CNC-machined test hardware. I bring a practical mindset to every project: designing not just for ideal performance, but for real-world constraints like budget, manufacturing feasibility, and timeline.

Beyond technical work, I've contributed to humanitarian design through Engineers Without Borders, building infrastructure for communities in Nepal — a reminder of why good engineering matters.
